How We Are Organised

Power flows from residents upward.

  1. Ward Community Assemblies

    Open to every resident. Elects ward councillors and Board representatives, sets ward priorities, and approves ward budgets.

  2. Municipal Community Assembly

    Delegates from every ward. The Movement's highest decision-making body — approves budgets and can amend the Constitution.

  3. Board of Governance

    Seven elected members serving three-year terms, acting as the Movement's legal entity and trustee.

  4. Council Representatives

    Elected councillors who report back to their Ward Assembly at least monthly and face annual performance review.

  5. Specialist Committees

    Expert advisory bodies covering infrastructure, finance, economic development, education, safety, and the environment.

  6. Council of Elders

    Respected community members providing wisdom, mediation, and mentorship to emerging leaders.

Guiding Principles

How we lead.

Inclusive Participation

All residents are welcome to become members by virtue of residence.

Democratic Governance

All leadership positions are filled through transparent, democratic processes.

Accountability

All leaders are accountable to the community and subject to recall.

Evidence-Based Decisions

Policies based on expert advice and community consultation.

Fiscal Responsibility

Transparent and responsible management of all resources.
