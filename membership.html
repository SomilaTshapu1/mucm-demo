Every resident of Makhanda is considered a member of the Movement — no application, no fee, no political test.

Membership

  1. Every person who is a resident of Makhanda is welcome to become a member of the Movement.
  2. Membership requires no formal application, fees, or specific political affiliation.
  3. Members have the right to participate in community meetings, vote in internal elections, and access Movement services.

Rights

  • Participate in ward meetings and community forums
  • Vote for ward councilors and Board representatives
  • Access information about Movement activities and finances
  • Propose initiatives and policies for community consideration
  • Hold leaders accountable through established mechanisms

Responsibilities

  • Contribute positively to community development
  • Respect the rights and dignity of all community members
  • Participate constructively in democratic processes
  • Support the values and objectives of the Movement
